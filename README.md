# HR-MIS-DASHBOARD
HR Mis Dashboard Creation of Depron Private Limited
# Overview
This repository contains an end-to-end HR Management Information System (MIS) Dashboard designed for Depron Private Limited. The dashboard provides comprehensive visibility into organizational
headcount, daily attendance trends, departmental performance metrics, designation breakdowns, and location-wise employee distributions across major hubs.
# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total HeadCount: 5000 Employees across 9 Metro locations
- Daily Attendance Rate:  86.60 overall average
- Present Today : 3,777 employees(76%)
- Absent Today: 395 employees (8%)
- On Leave Today: 573 employees (11%)
- Work From Home (WFH): 255 employees (5%)
# Core Features & Analytical Breakdown
1. Departmental Performance
Analyzes workforce metrics across 10 key business units (Operations, Sales, IT, Customer Support, MIS, HR, Admin, Marketing, Analytics, and Finance):
- Largest Departments: Operations (709 employees) and Sales (644 employees)
- Highest Attendance Average: IT (86.79%) and Analytics (87.02%)
2. Geographic & Workforce Distribution
- Locations Covered: Bengaluru, Chennai, Delhi, Gurugram, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai, Noida, and Pune
- Even Distribution: Each location maintains a balanced headcount between 526 and 578 employees, led by Bengaluru (578) and Delhi (570).
3. Designation & Role Hierarchy
- Executive Level: 1,429 employees
- Associate Level: 1,030 employees
- Senior Executive: 829 employees
- Analyst Level: 574 employees
- Management (Manager & Assistant Manager): 663 combined employees
4. Hiring & Tenure Trends (2019 – 2025)
- Peak Hiring Year: 2023 with 754 new joins
- Historical Baseline: Consistent onboarding averaging ~710 joins per year from 2019 through 2025.
